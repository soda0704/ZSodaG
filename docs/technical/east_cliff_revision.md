# East cliff and mountain decoration — 2026-10-06

The Dover scan was only a reference. Its imported mesh, texture, shader and collision resources were removed, along with export credits for that unused model. No scan is instantiated at runtime.

The original broad descending terrain slope was reshaped into a rocky drop. A native saved patch replaces the narrow wall: `scenes/art/environment/native_east_cliff.tscn`, instanced as `SnowExterior/EastCliffWall`. The replacement has 5,368 triangles (about 137 KiB for its native ArrayMesh), two irregular snow-covered ledges and broad rock buttresses. The static collision has the same vertices and triangles; both sides collide. Shape normals smooth along strata and retain the ledge edges.

The matching rectangle in Terrain3D has its collision hole flag painted. Stock Terrain3D hides hole vertices with NaN positions; coarse LOD triangles at that border could leave a comb of exposed strips. The saved Terrain3DMaterial now uses `terrain_cliff_cutout.gdshader`: finite vertices inside this replacement area and an exact fragment cutout, while other holes keep the original behavior. The render cutout is inset two metres from the authored patch bounds (Inspector parameter `cliff_replacement_bounds`, min/max X then min/max Z). Collision holes and the mesh collider remain unchanged. This source was generated by the installed Terrain3D autoshader, including existing auto texturing and projection; regenerate it and retain the local cutout when upgrading Terrain3D. Heights and hole flags are saved in `assets/environments/snow/expanded_terrain`; the shaft's existing hole remains. No runtime scenery generation or light changes were added.

Material references and snow/rock settings are editable on `LayeredRockFace` in Inspector. Terrain and saved wall are editable engine assets, not a per-frame procedural feature. The mesh uses the existing rock/snow shader and granite texture.

Upper decoration was removed together with collision shapes: 75 pine instances/colliders from upper slopes and the edited rim, four fallen pines (including the old rim placement), and nine high ice/rock dressing instances. Five lower fallen pines and twelve lower ice blocks remain. Low loose rock MultiMeshes were kept. Another 1,842 background billboard trees were removed from high foothills; the lower forest remains.

`scenes/art/environment/west_pass_forest.tscn` adds 100 shared pine instances near (-270, 32, 102) in eight MultiMesh chunks. It avoids high or steep ground and existing trunks, has native trunk collisions and participates in the existing VegetationDistance setting through exterior_chunk metadata. The earlier 150-tree southern pass also participates in this setting. Storm coverage and the previous 0.028 haze density are unchanged.

Validation: south_forest_cliff_regression.gd checks 96 seam support rays, cliff face collision, mask flag, both forest scenes' grounding and collisions, peak cleanup and retained storm settings. world_boundary_regression.gd checks world limits, nonlethal boundaries and retained lower dressing. The exterior was rendered from the front, both flanks and the new western forest point. Art direction remains a visual review, not an assertion of photorealism.

Seam correction validation: rendered additional views above the rim, along both directions, and near the foot with fog disabled; compared the same cameras with the old material. Regression verifies saved override and overlap bounds, shaft hole and 96 seam collision rays.
