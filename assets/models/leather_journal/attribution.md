# Leather journal

This work is based on "Leather journal" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/leather-journal-18f2248f31e6497f8e8bd9fd517f0137) by Jeant_220 (https://sketchfab.com/juanfranciscotorresvicuna), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Original supplied model and textures are preserved. Integration uses a native Godot presentation scene and AnimationPlayer.

The game presentation derives a separate articulated mesh from the original leather surfaces, removes both leather fastening tongues and the metal rivet, and fits the original page block beneath the reading sheets. It adds a hinged front cover, inner paper surfaces, a skinned interior paper fold and three curved leaves attached to the cover. The original source files remain unchanged.
