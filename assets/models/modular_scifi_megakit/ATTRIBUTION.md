# Modular SciFi MegaKit Standard

- Author: Quaternius.
- Original product: https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html
- Supplied archive: `Modular SciFi MegaKit[Standard].zip`.
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal. See the unchanged `License_Standard.txt` in this directory.

NorthernLab includes 34 selected glTF models and their 12 shared texture maps. Relative image paths were redirected to the shared texture directory; the AO channel was connected to the supplied ORM maps. Native Godot meshes, shared materials and matching static collisions are generated from those sources. Glass transparency is configured for Godot. Four ceiling objects rotate the original flat floor panels; three wall assemblies combine 3 m and 2 m source sections at 110% vertical scale to match the nominal 5.5 m height of floor zero. No source texture artwork was repainted.

The exact source entries and prepared object definitions are recorded in `selection.json`. Preparation and usage are documented in `scenes/objects/base/modular_scifi/README.md`.
