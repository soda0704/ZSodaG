# Modular SciFi MegaKit Standard

- Author: Quaternius.
- Original product: https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html
- Supplied archive: `Modular SciFi MegaKit[Standard].zip`.
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal. See the unchanged `License_Standard.txt` in this directory.

NorthernLab includes all 191 unique models: 190 glTF scenes and the OBJ-only small fan propeller, with 21 shared texture maps. Relative image paths were redirected to the shared texture directory; the incorrect buffer filename in Decal_Line_90 was corrected. Existing curated materials, including their ORM/AO and glass settings, are reused. New static models have native Godot meshes and matching static collisions; the five animated models retain their imported scene hierarchy, skeletons and animations. Decals and animated objects have no static collisions. Seventeen ceiling objects rotate the original flat platform panels without scaling. No source texture artwork was repainted, and no game level is modified by the library preparation.

The full source inventory is recorded in `library.json`; prepared scenes are indexed in `scenes/objects/base/modular_scifi/library_index.json`. The older `selection.json` describes the original 34-model subset only. Preparation and usage are documented in `scenes/objects/base/modular_scifi/README.md`.
