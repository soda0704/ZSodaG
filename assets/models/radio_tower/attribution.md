Modified for NorthernLab: extended and progressively tapered lattice sections, widened lower structure, continuous source ladder, cleared climbing well, original fence gate separated for animation, upper service deck and guard rails assembled with source materials, electrical cabinet moved to the platform, replacement cartridge derived from the source electrical box. UVs on new deck panels were authored to retain the original grating material. Source materials and textures are retained; embedded image names were normalized for repository naming rules.

The user-supplied 72 m cellular tower was inspected as a silhouette reference; its geometry and textures are not included in this asset.
