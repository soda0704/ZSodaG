# Dry flowers

From Lowpoly Fall Foliage Pack by EvanLegrand, supplied in lowpoly_fall_foliage_pack.zip. Original CC-BY-4.0 notice: license.txt. The two flower meshes are used in plant_stages/dead_6.tscn (bent DeadSunflower3) and dead_7.tscn (straight DeadSunflower2). Their original alpha textures and source credit are preserved. Materials are alpha-scissor, double-sided and non-emissive. Their shared warm-dry shader remaps the cold source photos into a straw palette; shadow/highlight colours and cutoff are editable in each material resource. The upper flower has a separate crossed photo surface to give it a little depth without duplicating the stem. The prepared stage bounds are centred and compressed in depth to fit the glass chamber (maximum 19.5 cm). Other unneeded source plants are not imported into the game.
